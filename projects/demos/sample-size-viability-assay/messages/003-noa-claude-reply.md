# Reply: plate facts and limits

## Summary

Thanks, the SD uncertainty point is fair and I had not considered it. Here are the practical limits of the experiment. The binding one is that we can run **at most 3 plates**.

## Content

**Plate facts, as confirmed by Noa:**

| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| Plate | 96-well |
| Edge wells | Not used (evaporation). **60 inner wells** per plate |
| Needed on every plate | 6 positive-control wells (staurosporine) and 6 medium-only blank wells |
| Left for samples | 60 − 12 = **48 wells** per plate |
| Maximum plates | **3**, because all plates must be seeded from the same cell passage |

**Consequences:** the usable sample wells are at most 3 × 48 = 144, far more than 17 per group. So plate space is not the limit. I do not yet know whether compound supply is, and I will ask Noa.
